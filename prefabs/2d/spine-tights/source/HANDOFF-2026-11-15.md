# 撕襪 2D (2026-11-15)

## Now: the Spine version (src/Main.ts); the shader version is in attic/

After the user found the shader version "比想像中差不少", the outfit was rebuilt as a Spine 4.2 rig with the
user's toolkit zhuda-spine2D (cloned read-only to engines/zhuda-spine2D; one local change: tools/capture.mjs
ignores the browser's own favicon 404, which failed every capture). Run the kit with
`PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=<a playwright-core>/index.mjs
CHROME_PATH="/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" python3 spk.py <cmd> spine/outfit`.

- `spine/outfit/make_parts.py`: body cut-out of the legs photo (flood-filled from the waist, no floor), the
  skirt photo and its dark back; `bg.png` for the game. `make_tights.py`: tights and tears from photos of real
  sheer black nylon over a white card (blender/nylon-*.png, gpt-image-2.6): brightness = transmission. The
  tights' density is the swatch's, denser where the leg turns away; 10 tear sites (5 a leg, below the hem);
  each tear is two decals clear outside the tear (bare leg where the knit opened; extra nylon at the rolled
  lip), so no seam however it is blended (an opaque baked patch showed bands in LayaAir: it blends sheer
  black differently from display values, and no single gamma matched).
- `spine/outfit/rig.py`: skirt = one mesh on 9 pleat chains (W.sheet) with physics, each with a 2-bone IK to
  a `grab_N` bone (mix 0); tights = a mesh weighted to one `pull_*` bone a site (narrow across the leg);
  slots `tear_*`/`lip_*` with small/large attachments. `spk check` ok; stress sheets in spine/outfit/evidence.
- Game: features core/2d/spine42. LayaAir's Spine2DRenderNode findings: it places the rig by its bounds
  (holder at the canvas's corner, and it sets skeleton.x/y itself: bone world positions must subtract them);
  set `useFastRender=false` (else bone and attachment changes do not show), `physicsUpdate = 2` (its default
  0 means Spine physics never steps), straight alpha. Spine 4.2 bones have no `worldRotationX` property
  (use `localToWorld`). Works on WebGL and WebGPU.
- Desktop real-mouse checks: tear (small then large), skirt lift with IK, stretch. Evidence:
  evidence/spine-sheet.jpg, gl-tears.png, gpu-tears.png.
- NOT checked on the V40: when I went to test, the phone was showing another game (port 8899, "Dragon
  Eggs"): someone else was using it; left alone.
- Open: the stretch leaves a pale band at the leg's edge; the swing after release is subtle (physics just
  switched on: tune strength/inertia); the atlas is 2048x4096 (32 MB GPU), worth shrinking for phones.

- 2026-11-15 later: `src/Main.ts` now uses the engine's `ZhudaSpineCharacter` (engine PR
  zhudaandzhuer/zhuda-laya-engine#7, fast-forwarded into main as `552736a`). The pin is now `#552736a`;

## Works (checked on desktop and on the vivo V40, real touch, 60 fps)
- Art (gpt-image-2.6-flare, direct line; prompts in `blender/`): `legs-bare.png` (waist to floor, black safety
  shorts, black pumps; her full-body front as reference), `skirt.png` (houndstooth pleated mini, ghost
  mannequin, transparent; its inner rim cropped off in `public/assets/art/skirt.png`). A tights version of the
  photo was refused by the image service's safety filter ("sexual", at output); not retried around the filter:
  the tights are drawn in the shader.
- `src/Main.ts`, orthographic camera (a tall screen shows the legs large, the grey sides cut):
  - `legData()` finds the legs in the photo (differs from each row's grey, not black, warm; specks below the
    ankles dropped) and how squarely each point faces us.
  - The legs shader: sheer black nylon (Beer-Lambert, dense at the edges, a sheen down the front, knit), its
    outline fixed to the leg.
  - Drag a leg: the knit thins round the finger and a nylon "tent" with threads stretches from the leg to the
    finger (a stiff spring while held, a springy return with overshoot). Past `tearAt` (0.17 of the picture's
    width) it tears: the bench's tear (`src/tear.ts`, copied) opens fast and its ladders creep; a pull near a
    hole widens it; up to 12 holes.
  - The skirt: `createZhudaClothGrid` 36x24 (the same on phones: a coarser grid settled into a balloon on the
    V40) + `ZhudaCloth` (drag 1.4: it swings after release), waistband rows pinned, a plane collider in front of
    the legs; the bench's 3x3 pinch, lifted toward the viewer as it rises.
- Query overrides: `denier` (20), `tearAt`, `skirtW` (0.72), `skirtTop` (0.04), `drag` (1.4), `dpr`.
- Evidence: `evidence/rest.png`, `stretch.png`, `torn*.png`, `lift.png`, `sw*.png` (desktop);
  `evidence/v40-rest.png`, `v40-stretch.png`, `v40-torn.png`, `v40-lift.png`, `v40-swing.png` (phone).
