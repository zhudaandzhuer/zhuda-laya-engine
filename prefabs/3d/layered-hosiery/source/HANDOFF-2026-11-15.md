# 撕襪實驗室 (stocking-tear), 2026-11-15

A test bench (not a game: no score, no clock, no goal; the user asked for it so) on zhuda-laya-engine, made from the engine's own 3D template (`zhuda-laya-init --template 3d`)
and pinned to `github:zhudaandzhuer/zhuda-laya-engine#ed1ddbc`. Not a git repository; nothing is committed.
It grew out of `films/knit-study` (the rebuild of `~/Desktop/nylon.html`): legs, pull and tear code come from there.

## Play

    npm install                          # once; the pinned SDK
    node tools/build.mjs                 # WebGPU (RENDERER=webgl node tools/build.mjs for WebGL; same dist/web)
    node tools/serve.mjs                 # http://127.0.0.1:8897/index.html

- Drag a thigh to pull what is on top there (shorts/trousers where they cover, else the stocking); pull far
  enough (the bar under the counters) and let go, or keep pulling, and it tears. Press inside a hole in a garment
  and you pull the stocking underneath.
- Drag a skirt's hem to lift it (a fold of particles moves together); drag empty space to turn round; wheel nearer.
- No score, timer, best time or finish screen (removed at the user's word, 2026-11-15): the top left only names
  what is worn. Right panel (a "換一雙" drawer on a narrow screen): 15 stocking kinds, colours, 10 bottoms, 復原.
- Ladders (runs) beside a tear: rungs about a millimetre apart (200 a leg unit, was 24 from the reference, which
  read as zebra stripes on the phone) and drawn as their average, a thinner band, where finer than a pixel;
  runs 0.6 as long as before, so the hole is what is seen.
- Page parameters for checks: `type=<id>`, `color=<number>`, `bottom=<id>`, `zoom`, `yaw`, `pitch`, `ty`.

## Bottoms (`src/bottoms.ts`)

Skirts are the engine's cloth, rebuilt per style (rings laid round the hips, a fitted one round the legs): A 字迷你裙,
百褶短裙, 窄裙, 飄逸長裙 (lifts up to six tenths of its length). Shorts and trousers are a garment layer over each leg
(same grid as the stocking, standing off the skin; pulled and torn the same way, no ladders; denim leaves white weft
across a rip): 牛仔短褲 (frayed), 破洞牛仔短褲 (loose, three ready-made rips, long fringe), 西裝短褲, 牛仔長褲,
西裝長褲. And 不穿. The user's reference pictures (2026-11-15) gave: all-over grey lace tights, a black lace top with
cross garter straps, loose ripped denim shorts, black sheer tights with short tailored shorts.

## Kinds of stocking (`src/patterns.ts`)

From the usual hosiery terms (denier bands, finishes, constructions; sources in the reply that made this):
超薄透膚 8D, 輕薄 20D, 半透 40D, 不透 80D, 厚實 120D, 亮面 15D, 後縫線 15D, 蕾絲邊大腿襪 20D, 蕾絲吊帶襪（十字）(wide floral lace top, leather straps with a cross), 蕾絲花紋褲襪 (floral lace all
over on a light net), 細網 Micronet, 經典網襪, 大網 Whale net, 羅紋褲襪, 點點 Plumetis. Each has its own tile (knit, diamond net, rib),
density, gloss, how hard it must be pulled (`tearAt`), how far it ladders (nets do not), hole size, and colours
(黑 膚 灰 白 酒紅 深藍, as fits the kind). A lace top ends at a scalloped edge with bare skin above; a back seam
shows from behind (turn round).

## What is engine and what is game

Engine (public API only): `ZhudaLitSurfaceMaterial` with sheen and subsurface, hook writing alpha/normal and
reading the view direction and vertex colour; `ZhudaDynamicMesh`; `ZhudaCloth` (skirt) with leg tables;
`ZhudaTriangleBvh` (picking); `ZhudaSceneLook`, `ZhudaHdrEnvironment` + studio room; the runtime clock
(`context.runtime.start`, world layer). Game: the catalogue, tiles, pull/tear rules, the round, the HUD (plain DOM).

## Sharpness on a phone (2026-11-15, vivo V40, WebGPU)

The picture looked soft: Laya's retina canvas was on, but this game capped the pixel ratio at 1.5 on phones
(canvas 617x1227 for a 1080x2400 screen, stretched up plainly). Measured, idle:

| Canvas cap | 3D picture | Edge smoothing | fps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.5 (old default) | full, plain stretch | FXAA | ~52, knit lost |
| 2 | full | FXAA | ~35 |
| 2.625 (native) | full | FXAA | ~23 |
| 2.625 | 0.6 + FSR | SMAA | ~35 |
| **2 (new default on phones)** | **0.75 + FSR** | **SMAA** | **~43 (34 while pulling), knit visible** |

FSR (the engine's `ZhudaRenderScale` through `look.setRenderScale`) is skipped under LayaAir's own FXAA, so phones
now use SMAA. The per-frame cost is in the pixels the GPU fills, not the simulation, nor the HTML panel. Overrides
for checks: `dpr`, `rs`, `up`, `aa`, `q`. Pictures: `evidence/v40/dpr-*.png`, `q-*.png`, `sharp-default.png`.

Tooling note: `mobile v40 open-url` drops everything after the first `&` of a URL (only the first parameter
arrives); navigate through DevTools (`tools/phone.mjs "location.href='...'"`) for URLs with several parameters.

## Skirt flicker when dragged (2026-11-15, found on the V40)

Seen in a screen recording of the phone: the grab moved a rigid 7x9 block of particles (the engine's hand holds
one particle, and a weighted patch of hands jittered because a hand waits whenever its threads stretch past 5%,
stretch 0.11 alternating frame to frame). On the phone's 64x14 cloth the block read as a flat lifted plate with hard
creases whose shading and cyan rim flipped each frame. Now: a 3x3 pinch held by the solver's pins (`pin`/`movePin`)
moved toward the finger at most 1.6 m/s, the cloth forms the fold (stretch 0.04 held); the skirt's sheen lowered.
Before/after: `evidence/v40/skirt-before-after.mp4`. Desktop lift checked (`bench-lift.png`).

## Leg proportions (2026-11-15)

The legs read as "pig legs": the reference's sections are for a close look at thighs, and against a slim adult
leg scaled to this model the knee was ~24% too wide, the ankle ~18%, the calf ~12%, the shin nearly straight; a
ripple copied from the reference (yarn behind the knee) showed as rings at the knees. Now `slimAt(y)` narrows
the sections by height (thighs as they were, knee 0.8, ankle 0.74-0.76), the kneecap is smaller, the ripple is
gone, and the feet are slimmer and a little longer. Before/after on the V40: `evidence/v40-slim.jpg`; a touch
tear still lands on the new shape. Still open: the tops of the feet look paler than the legs (they face the key
light and catch the sheen).

## Legs and feet from Blender (MPFB), 2026-11-15

The hand-built legs (`attic/body-handbuilt.ts`, kept for reference) were replaced by a modelled body:
- Tools: Blender 5.2.2 (installed) + MPFB 2.0.18 (MakeHuman for Blender, GPL-3 add-on, installed into Blender's
  `user_default` extensions from extensions.blender.org, sha256 c075b3e9...2dad; the zip is in
  `blender/downloads/`). The MakeHuman base mesh and what MPFB generates are CC0.
- `blender/export_legs.py` (run headless, command at its top): a slim adult woman (macro values in the file), shape
  baked, cut at the waistband (1.115 m) with arms removed, legs brought in below the crotch (ankles 20 cm apart),
  the crotch smoothed into a plain form (no anatomy), one level of subdivision, split at the midline into two legs
  with shared normals on the cut, written to `public/assets/legs.json` (about 8,100 vertices and 16,000 triangles a
  leg; ~1.1 MB). Re-run it to change the body.
- `src/meshleg.ts`: the pull, stretch, tears, tear origin, cloth bodies (`legTable`), hips for the skirt, garment
  layers and a crotch bridge, all per vertex (turn round the leg and height) instead of by grid rows. The texture
  goes round the leg (seam at the back, split with copied vertices); the toes are marked in the vertex colour for
  the reinforced toe; tights have a denser panty top from 6 cm below the crotch.
- Clothing heights in `bottoms.ts` / lace tops in `patterns.ts` were remapped to the new body (ankle -2.63, knee
  -0.86, thigh top 0.72 units).
- Checked: desktop WebGPU (tears both legs, held pull, jeans tears, skirt lift, lace tops, all bottoms), V40
  (~54 fps idle, was ~39; touch tears on both legs). `evidence/v40-mpfb.jpg` (old / new / new torn).
- Not checked since the switch: WebGL build, every stocking kind and bottom one by one, the garter strap position.

## Feet fixed (2026-11-15)

The feet showed jagged see-through "walls" and a broken knit. Causes, in order of how much they mattered:
1. MPFB joint places were read off the base mesh before the body shape was baked, so bringing the legs together
   moved the feet across the midline and the left/right split sliced each foot's inner side open. Joints are now
   measured after baking; the feet sit 6-14.5 cm from the midline.
2. The stocking shape is made in Blender as a shape key (`sock`): the skin pushed out 2.3 mm along its normals,
   smoothed over the toes (40 iterations, a weighted group) and shrinkwrapped "outside" over the toes only.
   Exported per vertex as `sock`; the game uses it as the stocking's rest shape (the code-side envelope is gone).
3. The knit cannot follow the foot's shape with this mapping; on the foot it is drawn as its average (fading in
   over the ankle). The texture goes round the leg as before; v goes on with the distance forward on the foot.
4. The stocking is drawn single-sided (it was double-sided: on a concave foot its inside showed through).
V40: ~55 fps, no renderer errors (`evidence/v40/feet-final.png`). `shot.mjs` takes `SCRIPT_FILE=` for longer
in-page checks; `ao=` turns ambient occlusion down for checks.

## Choices to know

- The skirt is plain black wool, not the reference's striped pleats (which read as a school uniform).
- No depth of field: the wheel changes the distance and the look's focus stays where it was made.
- TypeScript is 5.7.3, not the template's 5.6.3: the SDK's declarations use generic typed arrays (`Float32Array<...>`),
  which 5.6 cannot read, so the template as made fails `npm run typecheck`. An engine template finding.

## Engine findings from this game (not sent; the user said not to assign the engine session more work)

1. The 3D template pins TypeScript 5.6.3 with `skipLibCheck: false`, and the SDK's declarations need 5.7+:
   a fresh `zhuda-laya-init --template 3d` project fails its own `npm run verify` at typecheck.
2. The lit surface's shader-name contract includes each uniform's starting value, so two materials of one shader
   cannot start with different values ("Shader name has a different contract"): left and right stockings, or two
   kinds one after another. Worked round: every material is made with zeros and its values `set()` after.
3. A hook that calls `fwidth` (or `dFdx`) inside a branch or a function called from one compiles on WebGL and fails
   on WebGPU ("must only be called from uniform control flow"); the material then draws without its tears and the
   only sign is `renderer.errors`. Hook validation does not catch it. Worked round by passing the derivatives in.
4. (Known, scheduled as WP21-WP24) uniform arrays on the lit surface, the sun's shadow taken by a brighter light
   (the template itself says not to add a directional light; this game adds two dimmer rims), WebGPU on a failed
   compile, a patch grab for cloth (done here in game code).
