# Route: A rigged character for phones, textured from picture tiles

A skinned glTF creature or person of about 13,500 triangles with one material and four baked
textures, made from a signed-distance definition (shapes on a skeleton, clips as functions of
time) rather than in a DCC tool, its surface sampled from the repository's seamless picture tiles
(`textures/`), with two lighter levels; it plays in a film scene as a `model` node with
`animation` and `motion`. Checked only in software (SwiftShader) on a Linux container; no phone.

- **Id:** `rigged-phone-character`
- **Kind:** workflow
- **For:** making a skinned, animated character or creature as one textured GLB with two LOD levels for a phone game or film scene
- **Checked on:** desktop WebGL (Chrome's ANGLE on SwiftShader, Vulkan 1.3.0, Linux); no phone
- **Status:** ten beasts (饕餮 the template), five bosses, the scale figure and the scenes' three pilgrims built and checked on 2026-11-24 in software only (draft; written against an earlier engine commit and not re-run on this one)
- **Reference consumer:** the owner's verification repository (not published). The shape, skinning, clip and GLB tools it builds on are in this repository under `games/tools/characters/` (see `humanoid-from-shapes`); the textured path and the files named below are not: `epic-scenes/tools/beasts/` (`hd/textured.mjs`, `hd/convert.mjs`, `hd/tiles.mjs`, `beasts-hd/taotie.mjs`), `tools/beasts-lod/`
- **Engine version:** zhuda-laya-engine `6f467bba1b4a4052be66d4cee9b9346742a276dc`

## When to take this route

Take it for creatures and stylised people that are easier to describe as shapes on bones than
to sculpt, when there is no DCC artist, and the result must fit a phone (one draw a skin piece a
pass). If a modeller hands over a GLB, take `model-from-dcc` instead (steps 2 to 8 there still
apply). Not for hair cards, cloth simulation or facial rigs.

## Tools

- Node 22; Python 3 with xatlas in `tools/beasts-hd/.venv` (UV charts, `hd/unwrap.py`);
  meshoptimizer (simplification, the consumer's `package.json`); glTF Transform 4.5.1 and sharp
  in `tools/beasts-lod/` (LOD levels, texture size, JPEG, quantization); pngjs (reading tiles).
- The picture tiles in `textures/tiles/<id>/` (AI-generated by the engine's side, 512 and 1024,
  colour, height, normal; `textures/README.md`).
- Engine: `createZhudaSdfField`, `createZhudaIsosurfaceMesh` (the fine mesh), `ZhudaFilmHost`
  (`preview`, `review`, `flythrough`), the glTF intake (quantization, skins over 24 joints).
- Commit the definitions, tools, manifests (`index.json`, `lod.json`, `clip-check*.json`) and
  the preview sheets; the GLBs go to `deliverables/` by the assembly script.

## Steps

1. **Define the body.** Shapes (`E`, `C` round cones…) on named joints, a `paint` for colours,
   clips as `pose(t)` functions (`tools/beasts/kit.mjs`, `humanoid.mjs` for people:
   `tools/beasts/props/pilgrim.mjs`).
2. **Make it textured.** `toTextured(def, { zone, body, scale, tiles })` (`hd/convert.mjs`) or a
   hand-written `detail(p, n, shape)` and `surface(…)` (`beasts-hd/taotie.mjs`). Each zone of the
   surface (scales, fur, feather, horn, bone, shell, iron…) samples a tile through `hd/tiles.mjs`:
   - directional tiles (scales, fur, feathers) along the bone the shape hangs on: v down the
     bone, u round it in a whole number of tiles, the seam underneath; `horn` one tile along the
     whole piece; patterns without a grain (`bronze-thunder`) projected three ways;
   - colour = the tile's colour ÷ its mean × the definition's colour (linear), mixed 0.6 over the
     painted colour so the markings stay.
3. **Bake.** `node tools/beasts/make.mjs <id> --set <set> --preview --tag <t> --check`: fine mesh
   (cell 0.02 m), simplified to about 13,500 triangles, UV charts, skin weights, base colour 2048,
   normal 2048, ORM 1024, emissive 1024; one mesh, one material, no `COLOR_0`. Taotie: 187 s.
4. **No triangle reordering, then count.** Skins of more than 24 joints are ordered by the engine
   at load; the consumer's `laya-split.mjs` is off by default. Measure the draws (step 8): where
   the engine cuts more pieces than the consumer's order did (蚩尤, 40 joints: 3 and 4 a pass
   against 2), set `layaOrder: true` in the definition and pass `--order laya` to `lod.mjs`.
5. **Two lighter levels.** `node tools/beasts-lod/lod.mjs --in <folder with the one GLB> --out
   <set>/lod50 --ratio 0.5 --textures 1024` and `--ratio 0.25 --textures 512` (people for scenes:
   `--jpeg 88 --quantize`). Merge the written `lod.json` with the other characters' entries.
6. **Check every clip on every level.** `make.mjs <id> --set <set> --skip-build --check [--lod
   lod50|lod25]`: two moments of each clip side on must differ (Taotie: Idle 1.84, Move 2.85,
   Attack 2.69; the same within 0.05 on both levels).
7. **Compare with the last version.** Put the previous GLB back and draw it with the same views
   (`--skip-build --preview --tag r7`); sheets side by side.
8. **Count draws.** `review-work/beasts-hd/measure-draws.mjs`: the model alone over a floor, with
   and without shadows, last round's file beside this one's, every level (Taotie: 3 draws with two
   shadow cascades, 1 without; 13,498 / 6,748 / 3,374 triangles a pass; skins of 26 to 36 joints
   6 and 2; `review-work/round-8/draws-characters*.json`).
9. **Use it in a scene.** A `model` node with `animation` (`clip`, `loop`, `speed`, `offsetSec`)
   and, to walk, `motion` keys; far copies `castShadow: false`; the GLBs under `assets/`.

## Pitfalls

- **Strongly coloured tiles recoloured to another hue.** `tiger-fur` (orange and black) laid on
  窮奇's white cheeks went blue: ÷ an orange mean lifts the blue of the black stripes. Use a
  neutral tile (`white-fur`) and keep the markings in the definition's paint.
- **A tile's own pattern on top of painted markings is too busy.** Mix the tile's colour in at
  0.6, not all the way.
- **Tools that rewrite a manifest for one character.** `lod.mjs --in <one file>` writes a
  `lod.json` with only that one; `make.mjs --check <id>` writes `clip-check*.json` with only that
  one. Snapshot them first and merge (`review-work/round-8/rebuild-characters.sh`).
- **A moving model makes every shadowing point light redraw.** In a film scene any `animation`,
  `motion` or effect redraws all point-light shadows every frame (`ENGINE-REQUESTS-2.md` item 3).
- **A skin of 40 joints cut into more pieces by the engine's order.** 蚩尤 drew 9 a frame (12 at
  lod50) where round 7's file drew 6 (`ENGINE-REQUESTS-2.md` item 4). Keep `layaOrder: true` for it.
- **A clip too gentle for the check.** The scale figure's Idle (breathing 1.2°, head 6°) measured
  0.24 against the check's 0.3; 2.5° and 12° pass (0.43).
- **The parts with no tile.** `skin` and `flesh` have no picture in `textures/`; a body that is
  all skin (刑天, 燭龍, 蚩尤) looks almost the same as before.
- **The page's 50 MB.** Normal and ORM maps in PNG and float vertex data: JPEG them and quantize
  (`--jpeg 88 --quantize`; the three scene pilgrims went from 2.7 MB to 1.3 MB).

## Engine gaps and their workarounds

| Gap | Workaround |
| --- | --- |
| No quantization in `zhuda-laya-lod` (`ENGINE-REQUESTS-2.md` item 2) | `tools/beasts-lod/lod.mjs --quantize`, `quantize.mjs` (glTF Transform) |
| Point-light shadows redrawn every frame once anything moves (item 3) | No `castsShadow` on point lights in scenes with animated characters |
| The engine's skin order cuts a 40-joint skin into 3 to 4 pieces (item 4) | `layaOrder: true` and `lod.mjs --order laya` |

## Checks

- `make.mjs --check` on the full model and both levels: every clip "moves".
- The Khronos glTF validator (`tools/gltf-validate/validate.cjs`): 0 errors, 0 warnings on the
  quantized people.
- `measure-draws.mjs`: draws and triangles of the model alone (software drawing; counts only).
- In a scene: `film_scene_lint` advice empty, preview `metrics.flags` empty, no GL errors.

## Not checked

- Any phone, any frame time.
- WebGPU.
- The picture tiles' licence beyond what `textures/README.md` says (generated by the engine's
  side, not taken from any artefact).
- How the AI-generated tiles hold up close to a phone's screen at 3× pixel density.

## Boundaries

The consumer owns the definitions, the bake (`textured.mjs`, `convert.mjs`, `tiles.mjs`), the
LOD and quantization scripts and the manifests. The engine owns the glTF intake (dequantizing,
ordering skins), the `model` node with `animation` and `motion`, and its profiler's counts.
