# Route: a game, a screen or a film made with the three shared islands

The prefab library (`zhuda-engine-prefabs/`) holds three finished islands, each the same pixel for pixel as
the game it came from: the **floating island** above a sea of cloud (Zhuda can be picked up,
swung and thrown), the **home island** of the home page of the owner's site (the brother and
sister, Zhuda and Zhuer, wave, sing and dance), and the **garden island**, generated by code
(walked on in third person). A new game, a picture for the owner's site or a film starts here;
do not build an island again. Driven by mouse or touch; checked only in headless Chrome on a
desktop.

- **Id:** `shared-islands`
- **Kind:** scene
- **For:** A small island with weather, the hours of a day and characters that move (the floating island, the home island, the garden island), for a game, a home-page picture, a demo or a film.
- **Checked on:** desktop WebGPU (Apple M4, headless Chrome 158, 1280 by 720); no phone
- **Status:** 2026-11-25: the three islands were taken out of the original games and compared
  with them pixel by pixel, the same; the viewer's four scenes ran without errors.
- **Reference consumer:** `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/` (sky-island, island-pig, island-sound, garden-island,
  island-walker, zhuda-characters, nature-pack, day-look, quality-tiers),
  `zhuda-engine-prefabs/viewer3d/src/presets/` (sky-island, home-island, garden-island).
- **Engine version:** zhuda-laya-engine 1.18.0, `def94aae9e7ebf5f9f35b915b54dbb4c3e5fe43c`
  (pressed grass, the sea of cloud and the fog distance all need this version or a later one)

Everything on this page was done and checked on or before 2026-11-25, when the prefab library
was still in the ZhudaGameCenter workspace, beside the engine (outside this repository). The
paths are written as they are in this repository; nothing was run again from them.

## When to take this route

- Fits: a 3D scene of the small-island kind (seen whole, or walked on) that needs the light of
  a day and weather, with Zhuda or Zhuer active on it: a pet, a toy, a home page, a level-select
  screen, a short film.
- Does not fit:
  - A large map, a town, anything that needs terrain editing: take
    [film-as-data](film-as-data.md) and [good-looking-3d-scene](good-looking-3d-scene.md) (see
    the workspace's `films/river-town-prefab`).
  - Under water: [underwater](underwater.md).
  - A 2D game: [game-2d](game-2d.md).
- For how the floating island **was made** (the values of every step, and why):
  [floating-island](floating-island.md). This route is only about **using** the finished
  islands.

## Tools

- `zhuda-engine-prefabs/scripts/sync.mjs`: copies modules into a game. Models and sounds go unchanged to the
  game's `public/common/…`; their URL is `common/…`.
- `zhuda-engine-prefabs/viewer3d/`: the viewer; look first, then choose.
- The game's `zhuda.config.json`: `"features": ["core", "3d", "gltf", "postProcess"]`, with the
  engine pinned at `def94aa` or later.
- Filming: the owner's `zhuda-3d-film` agent skill (not in this repository): film mode, capture
  frame by frame, narration, mixing. The garden island's example storyboard is in
  `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/garden-island/source/`.
- Voices: the owner's `zhuda-voice` agent skill (not in this repository), with the voice
  `豬大_正式選定_C慵懶吐槽` for Zhuda and `豬二-妹妹_甜美少女` for Zhuer; the scripts of the lines
  are in `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/island-sound/source/`.
- Licences of the assets: the plants and stones are Poly Haven models (CC0); the song
  〈ふたりの帰り道〉 is the owner's own composition; Zhuda and Zhuer are the owner's own
  characters (their master files are in the owner's character folder, outside the workspace).

## Steps

1. **Look first.** `cd zhuda-engine-prefabs/viewer3d && npm run build && npm run view`, open
   `http://127.0.0.1:8930/index.html`, and click through the scenes and their tabs. Without
   running anything, look at `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/sky-island/preview/sheet.jpg` and
   `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/garden-island/preview/sheet.jpg`.
   - The floating island, `?scene=sky-island`: `?hour=18`, `?weather=rain|storm|mist`,
     `?cast=zhuda2|zhuer`, `?fx=low|mid|high|max`, `?off=fog,dof,…`, `?grass=0`, `?seed=`,
     `?governor=0`.
   - The home island, `?scene=home-island`: `?embed=1` (embedded in the home page),
     `?show=sing|wave|hands|talk|bow|sit`, `?clock=fast` (a day in five minutes).
   - The garden island, `?scene=garden-island`:
     `?view=path|pond|stream|beach|above|bamboo|terrace|dusk|night`, `?hour`, `?fx`, `?grass=0`,
     `?detail=0`, `?mirror=0`.
2. **Choose the island and the modules, and sync them into the game.**

   ```sh
   # from a checkout of this repository; <game dir> is the game's folder
   node zhuda-engine-prefabs/scripts/sync.mjs <game dir> 3d/sky-island 3d/island-pig 3d/island-sound 3d/zhuda-characters ui/scene-controls   # the floating island or the home island
   node zhuda-engine-prefabs/scripts/sync.mjs <game dir> 3d/garden-island 3d/island-walker 3d/zhuda-characters                             # the garden island and walking
   # or from the game, with the engine installed
   node node_modules/zhuda-engine-prefabs/scripts/sync.mjs . 3d/garden-island 3d/island-walker 3d/zhuda-characters
   ```

   Their dependencies (`3d/math`, `3d/day-look`, `3d/quality-tiers`, `3d/nature-pack`) are
   brought in with them.

3. **Write the game's own layer.** Copy the nearest preset as `src/Main.ts`:
   `zhuda-engine-prefabs/viewer3d/src/presets/sky-island.ts` (one pig, the interface), `home-island.ts` (two
   pigs, the performance commands, the home-page embed), `garden-island.ts` (walking). The
   shortest whole example is the section on putting it in a game (「放進遊戲」) of each module's
   README (the garden island's was put in an empty project, built and run).
   - The model library's provider is rooted at the page:
     `new DirectoryAssetProvider({ baseUrl: './' })`.
   - Each frame: the hour and the weather (the floating island's `createIslandDay()`; the
     garden island's own `hour`), then `skyAt(...)`, then the island's `update(...)`; pass the
     characters' feet to the island as `benders`, or the grass is not pressed down.
4. **Characters.** Grabbing, throwing, swimming and performing:
   `new Pig(model, island.world, …)` (`3d/island-pig`; the island is injected as a `PigWorld`).
   Walking in third person: `new Walker(model, world, start)` with `FollowCamera` and
   `WalkInput` (`3d/island-walker`; the island is injected as a `WalkWorld`, and the garden
   island is one itself). Models come from
   `characterPath('zhuda' | 'zhuer' | 'zhuda-watchman')`.
5. **Picture settings.** `makeTiers(SKY_ISLAND_GRASS | GARDEN_ISLAND_GRASS)`,
   `startTier(params)` (a phone starts low), and `ladder(ceiling)` for the engine's
   `ZhudaQualityGovernor`; the presets hold the whole wiring.
6. **Sound.** `createIslandSound({ provider, voices, song })`: no sound until the first click (a
   browser rule), ambience as streams, one spoken line at a time.
7. **An island of your own.** To put Zhuda on another island, implement `PigWorld` or
   `WalkWorld` for that island (height, how far inside the island, water, obstacles); the
   behaviour needs no change.
8. **The home page of the owner's site.** The steps that rebuild the home island are in
   `zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/sky-island/source/official-site/README.md` (the WebGL build,
   `register-official.mjs`). Always ask the owner before publishing.
9. **Check.** See Checks below; when the look has changed, take the module's `preview/`
   pictures again.

## Pitfalls

- **Do not run `npm run build` directly in the owner's site project (`officialSite`).** Its
  `dist` is a symbolic link to the live release, and a direct build overwrites the version that
  is on line (it happened once, on 2026-11-16). For a local trial build use
  `ASTRO_OUT_DIR=<temporary folder> npm run build`.
- **Asset URLs are not found.** After a sync the assets are at `common/3d/<module>/assets/…`;
  the provider's `baseUrl` must be `'./'`, not the `'./assets/'` of the old games.
- **A slow line on the first visit.** Sixty short sounds downloaded at once compete with the
  models for bandwidth, and past the deadline there is no sound. The shared module now loads 4
  at a time, the grab sound first, with a deadline of 60 s, and tries a failed one again without
  end; the models are tried again too, for about 4 minutes. Do not change it back to loading
  everything at once.
- **A character grabbed behind the island cannot be dragged to the front.** The drag plane is at
  the character's own depth. The home island's grab moves the plane slowly to the depth of the
  island's centre (`bringForwardTo` of `createHand()`), and a respawn lands on the side the
  camera can see.
- **Thin sheets (leaves, petals) are black from behind.** With the material's double-sided
  option the back face's normal is reversed; the garden island draws both the front and the back
  face, with the same normal.
- **A custom shader uniform must begin with `u_Zhuda`**, or creating the material throws and the
  page does not start (the garden island's sea).
- **A scene can have only one water mirror.** A pond and a sea cannot both be a
  `ZhudaWaterSurface`; the garden island's sea is a custom material, and its pond, stream and
  pool share one water surface.
- **Too many lights.** The garden island's 9 lights need `ZhudaEngine.boot({ maxLights: 16 })`.

## Engine gaps and their workarounds

| Gap | Workaround |
| --- | --- |
| No generator of trees or tree crowns | The garden island makes its crowns as an SDF isosurface with a layer of leaf cards outside (`zhuda-engine-prefabs/3d/garden-island/build.ts`, `detail.ts`) |
| No vertex colour or blended (splat) colour on the ground | Bake a 512 colour texture yourself (`build.ts`) |
| Only one water mirror in a scene | The sea is a custom surface on `ZhudaLitSurfaceMaterial` (`sea.ts`) |
| No character controller, and the camera has no collision with ordinary meshes | `WalkWorld` gives the ground and the area that can be walked; `FollowCamera` keeps clear of the tree crowns as spheres **After 2.0.0:** `ZhudaCharacterController` over a ground function and solids, and `zhudaSolidQueryProvider` for the camera boom ([walking](../walking.md)); meshes themselves are still not collided with, and the islands still use `WalkWorld`. |

Closed in 2.0.0: the headless Chrome host is a public entry
(`zhuda-laya-engine/compiler/chrome-host`); when the route was checked the tools imported the
installed package's file by path.

## Checks

```sh
cd prefabs && npm run verify     # 86 tests and the types (2026-11-25)
cd viewer3d && npm run verify    # sync, build, types, Doctor HEALTHY, tests
node tools/play.mjs scenarios/sky-hero.json "scene=sky-island&fx=high&governor=0"    # day, picked up, thunderstorm, dusk, night, morning mist
node tools/play.mjs scenarios/home-home.json "scene=home-island&embed=1&fx=high&governor=0"
node tools/play.mjs scenarios/garden-island.json "scene=garden-island&fx=high&governor=0"
```

The screenshots are in `evidence/` (`OUT=` changes that); `rendererErrors` and `pageErrors` at
the end of every output must be empty. The game must run its own `npm run verify` as well
(build, types, Doctor). For the pixel-by-pixel comparison with the original games see
[game-to-prefabs](game-to-prefabs.md).

## Not checked

- None of the three islands has been on a phone at engine `def94aa`; the vivo V40 numbers in the
  garden island's README were measured on the older engine `a5dfc6b`, and an iPhone was never
  tested.
- Nobody has listened to the sound (tone of voice, balance of volumes); unlocking sound on a
  phone was not tried.
- Mixed combinations were not run: Zhuda as a `Pig` on the garden island, or as a `Walker` on
  the floating island. The interfaces allow it, but neither was built or looked at.
- The rebuild for the owner's site went only as far as the WebGL client; the iframe URL in the
  site's `HomeIsland.astro` could not be read, and what the README gives is a presumed value.
- Touch was not tried with a real finger: walking on the garden island was tried with the
  DevTools touch events of headless Chrome, and the grab on the floating island was simulated
  with the script's `press`, `move` and `release`.

## Boundaries

- The game: its play, its levels, which island, how many characters, the content of the lines,
  the interface text, publishing. It writes only one thin `Main.ts`.
- The prefab library (`zhuda-engine-prefabs/`): the islands themselves, character behaviour, walking, sound,
  interface components and assets. To change an island's look or behaviour, change the source
  files in `zhuda-engine-prefabs/`, then sync again into every game that uses it and run its verification; do
  not change the copy inside a game.
- The engine: grass, water surfaces, particles, spring bones, light and shadow, the quality
  governor. A missing feature goes under the engine gaps above.
