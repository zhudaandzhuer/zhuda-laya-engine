## Limits and decision

The generic editor currently edits 2D `.lh/.ls` definitions. Images, audio,
models and other files are inventoried and safely served, while specialized 3D,
Spine and particle editing remain outside this contract. Native/mobile visual
acceptance is consumer-owned. Decision: **Implemented within scope**.
